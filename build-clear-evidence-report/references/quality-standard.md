# Report quality standard

Use this rubric for durable reports whose purpose is to explain complex work clearly. Score observable output, not effort spent.

## 100-point rubric

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit behavior |
|---|---:|---|
| Reader comprehension | 20 | In 30 seconds, the intended reader can state what the report is about and its main answer or state. In 3 minutes, they can explain the key reasoning. |
| Audience fit | 15 | Detail, vocabulary, examples, and consequences match the reader's knowledge and needs. Necessary technical names remain precise and are explained once. |
| Focus and hierarchy | 15 | The primary message comes first, each section answers one useful question, and detail is progressively disclosed without repetition. |
| Evidence credibility | 20 | Material claims are traceable; numeric definitions, sources, scope, method, and uncertainty are sufficient to interpret them. Facts, derivations, interpretations, and recommendations are distinguishable. |
| Simplicity | 10 | Removing any remaining paragraph, table, or chart would lose useful meaning. Labels are concrete and sentences avoid needless abstraction. |
| Data and visual expression | 10 | The chosen form answers a specific question; units, axes, denominators, precision, colors, ordering, and missing states are accurate and accessible. |
| Delivery quality | 10 | The requested artifact opens, the main reading path works, links and interactions are usable, and the report retains meaningful fallback content. |

## Release threshold

- **Ready:** at least 85/100 and no blocker.
- **Draft:** below 85, or semantically complete but awaiting a stated verification step.
- **Evidence-gap report:** the requested conclusion cannot be supported; clearly state what is known, unknown, and needed next.

Do not manipulate the score to reach the threshold. Record the weakest dimensions and correct the underlying report.

## Release blockers

Any one of these blocks a ready designation:

- A material conclusion has no evidence or contradicts the cited evidence.
- Facts, interpretations, and recommendations are presented as interchangeable.
- Incomparable data is ranked without an explicit normalization or caveat.
- Missing, untested, failed, and not-applicable states are collapsed into zero or one ambiguous marker.
- A material number lacks a unit, denominator, scope, time range, method, or source when the omission can change its meaning.
- A necessary explanation exists only in hover, color, an image, or JavaScript-rendered content.
- A required link, section, table, or interaction is broken in the delivered artifact.
- The report claims a test, source review, calculation, or browser verification that was not performed.

## Scoring procedure

1. Identify the intended reader and reader outcome at the top of the review notes.
2. Score each dimension independently and cite one observable reason for any deduction of 3 or more points.
3. Check blockers separately; a high numeric score cannot override one.
4. Correct the highest-impact weakness first: unsupported conclusion, unclear main message, unfair comparison, then presentation defects.
5. Re-score only after the artifact changes.
